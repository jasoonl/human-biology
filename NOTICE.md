# Third-party content

The bones, muscles, organs, brain, blood vessels, nerves and the body surface in this project are derived from
**Z-Anatomy** (https://www.z-anatomy.com), *The libre 3D atlas of anatomy*, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, which is itself
built on **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed **CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**
(https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html).

The meshes under `Assets/Generated/Meshes/` whose names start with `Z_`, and the skin meshes, are adaptations of that
data (repositioned and scaled, merged, welded and re-shaded; vessels and nerves rebuilt as tubes along the atlas'
centre lines; the skin computed from the atlas structures). Under the share-alike terms, those adaptations are
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 too. Structures the atlas does not contain (female reproductive organs, some heart internals) are
this project's own sculpts.

See `Tools/ZAnatomy/README.md` for how the data is exported and mapped.
